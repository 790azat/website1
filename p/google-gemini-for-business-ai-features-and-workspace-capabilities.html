Google Gemini for Business: AI Features and Workspace Capabilities

Michael Anderson
Business and Data Analyst · August 15, 2026

Google Gemini for Business is a collection of AI capabilities integrated into Google Workspace and the broader Google Cloud ecosystem. It provides AI assistance for tasks such as writing, research, data analysis, document creation, meeting summaries, communication, and workflow automation.

Rather than operating only as a standalone chatbot, Gemini can work directly inside applications that businesses already use, including Gmail, Google Docs, Sheets, Slides, Drive, Meet, and Chat. Google has also expanded Gemini into Notebook, Workspace Studio, and AI-powered agents for more specialized business workflows.

Google Workspace now includes Gemini capabilities across its business subscription structure, although the specific features available depend on the Workspace edition. This means businesses can evaluate Gemini as part of their overall productivity and collaboration environment rather than as a separate AI application.

What Is Google Gemini for Business?

Google Gemini is Google's family of generative AI models and applications. Within Google Workspace, Gemini acts as an AI assistant that can work with information available through authorized Workspace applications.

The assistant can help users draft and refine content, summarize information, analyze data, generate images, organize files, and prepare meeting notes. Gemini can also interact with information across Workspace applications through the side panel in supported products.

Google emphasizes that Workspace data is protected by existing organizational controls and that company data is not used for AI model training or advertising in the Workspace business environment.

Gemini in Gmail

Gemini provides several AI features inside Gmail.

Users can ask Gemini to summarize long email threads, draft messages, improve writing, suggest replies, and obtain summaries from Gmail search results. Google also provides features such as Help me schedule, which can suggest meeting times based on participant availability.

Gmail can therefore become an AI-assisted workspace for both communication and information retrieval. Employees can spend less time manually reviewing long conversations and more time acting on the information they contain.

Google has also introduced Gmail Live, a voice-oriented experience that allows eligible users to search and interact with inbox information using natural-language voice requests.

Gemini in Google Docs

In Google Docs, Gemini can help employees create, rewrite, summarize, and refine documents.

Users can generate initial drafts such as project plans, reports, or campaign briefs and then refine the content directly within the document. Gemini can also help with proofreading, formatting, and adapting content to specific styles.

Newer capabilities allow Gemini to use relevant information from Gmail, Drive, Chat, and the web when generating content, subject to the user's access and the feature's availability.

These capabilities can support business writing without requiring employees to move information into a separate AI application.

Gemini in Google Sheets

Gemini in Sheets provides AI assistance for data organization and analysis. Users can create tables, generate formulas, summarize information, identify insights, and build charts through natural-language requests.

Google has also expanded Gemini's spreadsheet capabilities so that eligible users can create new spreadsheets and perform end-to-end tasks on existing Sheets files using prompts.

For business teams, this can be useful for activities such as sales analysis, budget tracking, project management, marketing reporting, and operational data analysis.

Gemini in Google Slides

Gemini can assist with presentation creation in Google Slides. Users can generate slide content, rewrite existing material, and create images or other visual assets based on prompts.

This can help employees turn business information into presentations without manually creating every piece of content from scratch. Google's Workspace documentation identifies image generation and AI-assisted slide creation among the available Slides capabilities.

Gemini in Google Meet

Gemini also provides AI assistance for meetings.

The Take notes for me feature can automatically capture meeting notes and highlights. Gemini can also provide translated captions and, in supported configurations, real-time speech translation.

Google has expanded meeting-related functionality so that automated recaps can include summaries, key decisions, action items, and links to meeting notes and transcripts.

These features can help employees review meetings without relying entirely on manual note-taking.

Gemini in Google Drive and Chat

Gemini in Google Drive can summarize files and folders, answer questions about documents, and help users find relevant information. This can be useful when employees need to locate information across large collections of business files.

In Google Chat, Gemini can summarize conversations, identify action items, answer questions about chat history, and assist with information from conversations and events.

Together, these capabilities extend AI assistance beyond individual documents and into broader collaboration workflows.

Gemini Notebook

Gemini Notebook is designed to help users work with collections of trusted sources.

Businesses can use notebooks to organize source material and generate insights, summaries, and audio overviews based on those materials. Google states that enhanced Workspace versions include features such as notebook customization, sharing, usage analytics, and expanded capacity.

This can be useful for research, project documentation, internal knowledge sharing, and reviewing large collections of business information.

Google Workspace Studio and AI Automation

Google Workspace Studio allows businesses to create AI-powered workflows across Workspace applications.

For example, an organization can create automated flows for drafting emails, organizing tasks, processing information, and coordinating recurring activities. Google also supports AI-powered steps within these flows on eligible Workspace editions.

Google has additionally introduced integrations that allow agents to work with third-party applications such as Asana, Mailchimp, Slack, and Salesforce.

This expands Gemini from content generation into workflow automation.

Gemini Business Plans and Pricing

Google's current Workspace pricing structure includes Business Starter, Business Standard, Business Plus, and Enterprise.

The standard prices currently shown by Google are:

  • Business Starter: $7 per user per month
  • Business Standard: $14 per user per month
  • Business Plus: $22 per user per month
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing

Google currently shows promotional discounts for new customers, so the introductory amount can differ from the standard price.

Business Starter includes Gemini in Gmail and access to the Gemini app. Business Standard expands Gemini capabilities across Gmail, Docs, Meet, and other Workspace applications and adds expanded Gemini Notebook access. Business Plus provides additional storage and enterprise-oriented controls, while Enterprise adds advanced security, data governance, agent management, and other organizational capabilities.

Gemini for Different Business Teams

Sales teams can use Gemini to draft outreach, summarize customer information, prepare presentations, and organize meeting follow-ups.

Marketing teams can create campaign briefs, content drafts, presentations, and research summaries.

Finance teams can use Gemini in Sheets to analyze financial data, generate formulas, and identify patterns.

Human resources teams can draft employee communications, summarize information, and organize documents.

Project managers can use Gemini to create project plans, summarize discussions, organize tasks, and automate recurring workflows. Google specifically provides role-oriented Gemini resources for sales, marketing, HR, project management, and other business functions.

Security and Business Data

Google states that Workspace with Gemini provides enterprise-grade security and privacy and keeps users and organizations in control of their data. Gemini operates within existing Workspace permissions, meaning access to organizational information remains tied to the user's authorization.

Businesses should nevertheless establish internal policies for AI use, sensitive information, human review, and data governance.

How Businesses Evaluate Gemini

Businesses evaluating Gemini should consider which Workspace applications employees use most frequently, the amount of AI assistance required, data and security requirements, and the need for automation or third-party integrations.

The Workspace edition is also important because Gemini capabilities vary between Starter, Standard, Plus, and Enterprise plans.

Organizations should evaluate not only the AI features themselves but also storage, meeting capabilities, security controls, administration, and other Workspace functionality included with each subscription.

Final Thoughts

Google Gemini for Business brings generative AI into Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Drive, Meet, Chat, and other Google Workspace services.

Its capabilities range from drafting and summarization to spreadsheet analysis, meeting notes, research, AI-powered workflows, and agent-based automation. Google's continuing expansion of Gemini across Workspace also makes the platform increasingly relevant to organizations looking to integrate AI directly into everyday productivity tools.

For businesses evaluating Gemini, the main considerations are Workspace edition, AI requirements, organizational data, security controls, automation needs, third-party integrations, and total subscription costs.
