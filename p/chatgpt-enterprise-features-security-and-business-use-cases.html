ChatGPT Enterprise: Features, Security, and Business Use Cases

Daniel Brooks
Financial Specialist · August 23, 2025

ChatGPT Enterprise is OpenAI's managed ChatGPT offering for organizations that want to deploy AI across employees with centralized administration, enterprise security, and organizational controls. It combines ChatGPT capabilities with workspace management, identity controls, data governance, analytics, and access to advanced tools.

Enterprise users can work with ChatGPT for research, writing, analysis, coding, data processing, and other business activities. Current Enterprise workspaces also provide access to features such as Projects, GPTs, Apps, Company Knowledge, ChatGPT Agent, Deep Research, and Codex, depending on the organization's seat type and workspace configuration. (OpenAI Help Center)

The platform is purchased at the organization level rather than by individual users. This makes its administration and security model different from consumer ChatGPT subscriptions.

What Is ChatGPT Enterprise?

ChatGPT Enterprise provides organizations with a dedicated ChatGPT workspace where administrators can manage users, permissions, tools, models, applications, and other settings.

Employees can use the workspace for everyday productivity tasks as well as more advanced workflows. Organizations can also connect approved business applications and sources so employees can work with information from their existing systems.

Enterprise workspaces are separate from personal ChatGPT workspaces, and organizational policies govern content, access, retention, and feature availability within the managed environment. (OpenAI Help Center)

Key ChatGPT Enterprise Features

Advanced AI and Reasoning

ChatGPT Enterprise provides access to advanced ChatGPT models and higher-usage workflows intended for organizational use.

Employees can use AI for drafting, summarization, research, analysis, coding, brainstorming, and other knowledge-work tasks. Administrators can control which models and capabilities are available to different users or groups. (OpenAI Help Center)

This allows organizations to provide a common AI environment while maintaining administrative control over how it is used.

Deep Research

Deep Research can conduct multi-step research and synthesize information into detailed outputs. This can support activities such as market research, competitive analysis, technical research, and preparation of business reports.

Enterprise administrators can control access to research-related capabilities through workspace permissions and roles.

Data Analysis

ChatGPT can work with uploaded business files and structured datasets to analyze information, identify patterns, create calculations, and produce visualizations.

This can support finance, operations, marketing, sales, and other teams that regularly work with spreadsheets, reports, and datasets.

Projects and GPTs

Enterprise users can organize related work within Projects and create custom GPTs for specialized workflows.

Custom GPTs can use instructions and approved knowledge sources to provide more consistent assistance for specific organizational tasks. Enterprise administrators can manage GPT creation, sharing, access, and other controls across the workspace. (OpenAI)

Company Knowledge and Connected Apps

ChatGPT Enterprise can connect employees with approved business information through Apps and Company Knowledge.

Organizations can configure access to supported applications while maintaining the permissions established in the underlying systems. Enterprise administrators can control which apps are available and can use role-based access controls for appropriate users or groups. (OpenAI Help Center)

This can allow employees to ask questions about information distributed across approved business systems instead of manually searching multiple applications.

ChatGPT Agent and Codex

Enterprise workspaces can provide access to ChatGPT Agent, which can perform more complex multi-step tasks using available tools and connected services.

Codex provides AI assistance for software development and coding workflows. Current Enterprise plans can include standard ChatGPT seats with Codex access, while OpenAI also offers dedicated Codex seats under its credit-based Enterprise arrangements. (OpenAI Help Center)

These capabilities extend the platform beyond conversational assistance into more action-oriented and technical workflows.

Enterprise Security

Security is a central component of ChatGPT Enterprise.

OpenAI states that business data is encrypted at rest and in transit. The company also states that it does not use ChatGPT Enterprise workspace content to train its models by default. Enterprise customers can additionally use controls such as enterprise key management and configurable data-retention options. (OpenAI)

OpenAI also provides enterprise identity and access controls, allowing organizations to manage who can access the workspace and which capabilities are available.

Identity and Access Management

ChatGPT Enterprise supports enterprise identity-management features including single sign-on (SSO), domain verification, and SCIM provisioning.

SCIM can automate the creation and removal of user accounts, while SSO allows employees to authenticate through an organization's existing identity provider.

Role-based access control provides another layer of administration. Enterprise organizations can create custom roles and assign permissions for features such as GPTs, Projects, connected tools, Workspace Agents, and other capabilities. (OpenAI)

Data Privacy and Governance

OpenAI states that customer content from ChatGPT Enterprise is not used to train OpenAI models by default.

Organizations can also use data-retention controls and, for eligible customers, data residency options. OpenAI currently lists supported at-rest data residency locations including the United States, European Union, United Kingdom, Japan, Canada, South Korea, Singapore, Australia, India, and the United Arab Emirates. Availability depends on eligibility and product configuration. (OpenAI)

These controls can be important for organizations with internal data-governance policies or regional requirements.

Compliance and Audit Controls

Enterprise provides tools for organizations that need to monitor and govern AI usage.

OpenAI provides analytics and usage insights so administrators can examine adoption and engagement. Enterprise organizations can also use compliance-related capabilities and APIs for auditing workspace activity and managing organizational data. (OpenAI)

Administrators should configure retention, access, logging, and role settings according to the organization's regulatory and security requirements.

ChatGPT Enterprise Business Use Cases

Marketing teams can use ChatGPT for research, content development, campaign analysis, and customer insights.

Sales teams can summarize account information, prepare communications, analyze sales data, and conduct research.

Finance teams can analyze spreadsheets, prepare reports, summarize financial information, and support recurring analytical workflows.

Human resources teams can draft internal communications, summarize documents, develop training materials, and organize HR information.

Software teams can use ChatGPT and Codex for coding, debugging, documentation, and development workflows.

Management teams can use research, analysis, reporting, and organizational knowledge tools to support decision-making and planning.

ChatGPT Enterprise Administration

Workspace owners and administrators can manage members, groups, seats, permissions, models, GPTs, apps, analytics, and other workspace settings.

The Enterprise administration environment also allows organizations to configure access to tools and features according to specific roles. For example, an organization can provide different capabilities to engineering, finance, or marketing groups rather than giving every employee identical access. (OpenAI Help Center)

This structure can help organizations introduce AI gradually while maintaining centralized governance.

ChatGPT Enterprise Pricing

ChatGPT Enterprise does not have a publicly listed standard per-user price. Organizations purchase Enterprise through OpenAI's sales process, with pricing and billing arrangements determined by the agreement.

Current Enterprise agreements can use different seat and usage models. OpenAI introduced a Codex seat type in 2026 and also supports credit-based pricing for certain Enterprise arrangements. Organizations should therefore obtain a current quote and confirm which billing model applies to their deployment. (OpenAI Help Center)

Total cost can depend on the number of users, seat types, usage requirements, additional credits, and negotiated commercial terms.

How Businesses Evaluate ChatGPT Enterprise

Organizations evaluating ChatGPT Enterprise should consider the number of employees, intended AI use cases, existing software ecosystem, security requirements, data residency needs, identity infrastructure, and governance requirements.

Businesses should also determine which groups need advanced tools such as Deep Research, Apps, Company Knowledge, ChatGPT Agent, or Codex.

For larger deployments, implementation planning should include identity management, role definitions, approved applications, data policies, retention requirements, employee training, and usage monitoring.

Final Thoughts

ChatGPT Enterprise provides organizations with a managed environment for deploying ChatGPT across business teams. Its capabilities extend from everyday writing and analysis to research, coding, connected business data, AI agents, and specialized workflows.

Enterprise security features include encryption, SSO, SCIM, role-based access controls, data-retention options, and organizational controls for connected applications. OpenAI also states that Enterprise workspace content is not used to train its models by default.

For businesses evaluating the platform, the main considerations are AI use cases, workforce size, security and governance requirements, connected data, administration, usage expectations, and the commercial terms of the Enterprise agreement.
