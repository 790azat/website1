University of Illinois iMBA: Tuition, Curriculum, and Admissions

James Mitchell
Financial Specialist · January 20, 2026

The University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign offers the iMBA, an online Master of Business Administration through the Gies College of Business. The program is designed for working professionals who want to earn a graduate business degree through a flexible online format while continuing their careers. The iMBA requires 72 credit hours and combines core business education with specialized focus areas and electives.

The program differs from a traditional fixed-schedule MBA by allowing students to control the pace and sequence of much of their coursework. Individual courses generally run for four to eight weeks, and students can take between one and four courses during a 16-week semester. Gies states that the degree can be completed in approximately two to five years, depending on the student's pace.

The iMBA also integrates Coursera into the academic experience. Students use both Canvas and Coursera for their courses, combining university-level instruction with online learning materials and interactive coursework.

What Is the University of Illinois iMBA?

The iMBA is the online MBA program offered by the Gies College of Business at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. The university describes it as a flexible program designed around mobility, convenience, and the needs of students who prefer online education.

Students work through core business subjects before selecting specialized areas that can be aligned with their professional interests. The curriculum covers leadership, economics, finance, accounting, marketing, operations, analytics, and strategy.

The program also emphasizes teamwork and applied business problem-solving. Students participate in virtual study teams that draw on the different academic and professional backgrounds of their classmates.

iMBA Curriculum

The iMBA requires 72 credit hours for the standard degree path. Students complete four required core specializations before choosing additional focus areas or electives.

Strategic Leadership and Management

The first core specialization focuses on leadership and organizational management. Courses include Leadership & Teams, Managing Organizations, and Strategic Management, followed by a specialization capstone.

The curriculum addresses how managers organize teams, make strategic decisions, and lead organizations through changing business conditions.

Managerial Economics and Business Analysis

The second core area covers quantitative and economic decision-making.

Courses include Statistics for Management Decision Making, Microeconomics for Business, and Money and Banking. These subjects provide a foundation for interpreting economic conditions and using quantitative information in business decisions.

Value Chain Management

The third core specialization combines marketing, operations, and managerial accounting.

Students study Marketing Management, Operations Management, and Managerial Accounting, giving them an integrated view of how businesses create, deliver, and measure value.

Financial Management

The fourth core area focuses on financial decision-making.

Required subjects include Accounting Measurement, Reporting, and Control, Investments, and Corporate Finance. This specialization develops knowledge that can be applied to investment decisions, financial reporting, and corporate financial strategy.

iMBA Focus Areas

After completing the required core curriculum, students can customize the degree through focus areas and electives.

Gies currently lists six primary focus areas:

  • Digital Marketing
  • Entrepreneurship and Strategic Innovation
  • Global Challenges in Business
  • Business Analytics
  • Mergers and Acquisitions
  • Healthcare Innovation, Design, and Entrepreneurship

Students can complete two focus areas or combine one focus area with 12 credit hours of electives or an approved graduate certificate.

Business Analytics

The Business Analytics focus area covers applied analytics, communicating with data, business data modeling, predictive analytics, and applications in marketing and accounting.

This option can be relevant to professionals who work with reporting, business intelligence, forecasting, and data-driven decision-making.

Digital Marketing

The Digital Marketing focus area includes Digital Marketing Analytics, Digital Media & Marketing, and Marketing in Our New Digital World.

It can provide additional training for professionals working in digital marketing, customer acquisition, marketing analytics, and online business strategy.

Entrepreneurship and Strategic Innovation

This focus area examines strategic innovation, creative thinking, and entrepreneurship from startup development through growth.

It can be used by professionals interested in launching businesses, developing new products, or managing innovation within established organizations.

Electives and Graduate Certificates

Students who choose the elective pathway can select 12 credit hours from available iMBA focus-area and elective courses.

Current elective examples include Risk Management and Innovation, Disruption and Emerging Technologies, AI in Business, Project Management, and Infonomics.

The iMBA also allows degree-seeking students to earn eligible embedded graduate certificates while completing the MBA. Available certificate areas include Business Analytics, Digital Marketing, Entrepreneurship and Strategic Innovation, Global Challenges in Business, Healthcare Innovation, and Mergers and Acquisitions.

This structure gives students an opportunity to develop a more specific academic credential alongside the MBA.

Online Course Format

The iMBA uses relatively short courses rather than traditional semester-long classes.

Gies states that courses generally run four to eight weeks, while students can take between one and four courses during a 16-week semester. This allows learners to adjust their course load around professional commitments.

The program also uses both Canvas and Coursera. Students must successfully complete the associated Coursera coursework for their registered university courses, with the Coursera components forming part of the academic requirements.

Gies recommends budgeting approximately 10 to 15 hours per week for each registered course, depending on the course and workload.

University of Illinois iMBA Tuition

For the 2026–2027 academic year, the University of Illinois lists iMBA tuition at $379 per credit hour. The standard program requires 72 credit hours, producing an estimated total tuition of $27,288 before any additional eligible costs.

The university notes that annual inflation adjustments to tuition are possible. Most learning materials are included in tuition, although students may occasionally need to purchase a textbook or case study. Immersion, alumni, and networking activities are not included in the listed tuition and may involve additional costs.

The iMBA is classified as a self-supporting, cost-recovery program, and students generally are not eligible for tuition and fee waivers except for applicable statutory waivers.

iMBA Admissions Requirements

Applicants normally need a bachelor's degree from an accredited institution in the United States or an equivalent degree from an accredited institution in another country.

Gies currently recommends a 3.0 GPA on a 4.0 scale and at least three years of full-time, substantial work experience. However, the admissions team reviews applications holistically and can consider professional experience, references, and the applicant's personal statement.

Applicants generally submit academic records and the required application materials specified by Gies. International applicants may need to provide documentation establishing academic equivalency and satisfy applicable Graduate College requirements.

GMAT and GRE Requirements

The iMBA does not require GMAT or GRE scores for admission. Applicants can optionally submit a score, but Gies states that applicants are not penalized for choosing not to provide one.

This allows applicants to focus their application on academic history, professional experience, references, and their personal statement rather than requiring a standardized admissions test.

Applicants without an undergraduate degree can also explore the Performance-based Admissions Track (PAT). This provides an alternative route in which learners can demonstrate their academic ability through successful completion of specified coursework before progressing into the degree program.

Program Completion and Capstones

Students must complete the required core curriculum, focus areas or electives, specialization capstones, and the final program capstone.

The standard program requires completion of 72 credit hours and a minimum cumulative GPA of 2.75. Students can generally take up to five years to complete the degree while maintaining satisfactory academic progress.

Gies describes the program as typically taking two to three years, although the flexible structure allows students to extend their studies when necessary.

Final Thoughts

The University of Illinois iMBA combines a 72-credit graduate business curriculum with a flexible online structure. Students complete four core business specializations and then customize their education through focus areas such as Business Analytics, Digital Marketing, Entrepreneurship, Global Challenges, Mergers and Acquisitions, and Healthcare Innovation.

For the 2026–2027 academic year, tuition is listed at $379 per credit hour, producing an estimated $27,288 in tuition for the standard 72-credit program. Additional costs can apply to certain materials and optional experiences.

Admission generally requires a bachelor's degree, recommended 3.0 GPA, and at least three years of full-time work experience, although Gies states that applications are reviewed holistically. The GMAT and GRE are not required.
