Syracuse University Online MBA: Tuition, Curriculum, and Admissions

Daniel Brooks
Financial Specialist · December 18, 2024

Syracuse University's Martin J. Whitman School of Management offers an Online MBA designed for working professionals seeking a flexible graduate business education. Beginning in fall 2026, Whitman redesigned the program from 54 credits to 45 credits, reducing the amount of coursework required while allowing students to complete the degree in as little as 15 months.

The program is delivered fully online and combines a foundation in core business subjects with the option to specialize in areas including accounting, business analytics, entrepreneurship, finance, marketing management, and supply chain management. Students can participate in live online classes while maintaining professional responsibilities.

The 2026 redesign also allows eligible students to receive up to six credits for prior learning and relevant professional or leadership experience, potentially reducing the program to as few as 39 credits after an individual assessment.

What Is the Syracuse Online MBA?

The Syracuse Online MBA is offered through the Whitman School of Management and is accredited by the Association to Advance Collegiate Schools of Business (AACSB).

The current program contains 45 graduate credits and is structured around a core business curriculum plus specialization coursework. Whitman states that the curriculum is continually updated to reflect changing business trends and professional requirements.

Students can choose among three entry periods each year: fall, spring, and summer. Classes are offered at flexible times, with live sessions available during evening hours to accommodate working professionals.

Online MBA Curriculum

The curriculum is designed to develop broad management capabilities before allowing students to build additional expertise in a selected business discipline.

Core areas address topics such as:

  • Accounting and financial reporting
  • Managerial finance
  • Marketing management
  • Operations and supply chain management
  • Data analysis and decision-making
  • Information technology and e-commerce
  • Microeconomics
  • Strategic management
  • Leadership
  • Global business
  • Entrepreneurship
  • Business law and ethics

The program is intended to develop skills in organizational resource management, communication, critical thinking, quantitative analysis, strategic thinking, and global business.

MBA Specializations

Students can customize their degree through six specialization options.

Accounting

The accounting specialization provides additional exposure to financial reporting, accounting practices, and financial decision-making. It can be relevant to professionals whose careers involve corporate accounting or financial management.

Business Analytics

The Business Analytics specialization focuses on using data and analytical methods to support business decisions. It can be useful for professionals working in business intelligence, reporting, analytics, forecasting, and data-driven management.

Entrepreneurship

The entrepreneurship specialization develops knowledge related to identifying opportunities, developing business ideas, managing growth, and applying entrepreneurial thinking within organizations.

Finance

The finance specialization provides additional study in financial decision-making and related areas. It can complement careers in corporate finance, financial management, investment-related functions, and other business roles.

Marketing Management

The marketing management specialization addresses marketing strategy and customer-focused business decisions. It can be relevant to professionals working in marketing, brand management, customer strategy, and commercial operations.

Supply Chain Management

The supply chain specialization focuses on managing processes involved in sourcing, production, logistics, and distribution. It can support professionals working in procurement, operations, supply chain planning, and related functions.

Program Format and Completion Time

The redesigned Online MBA can be completed in as little as 15 months. The exact completion time depends on the student's course load and academic plan.

The program is designed for professionals who want to study without relocating or leaving their existing jobs. Live online classes are offered at flexible times, while coursework can be accessed through Syracuse's online learning environment and mobile application.

Students may also participate in optional in-person residencies. These weekend-style experiences focus on current business topics and can provide opportunities for workshops, networking, and interaction with classmates and business leaders. The program can also be completed entirely online from start to finish.

Syracuse Online MBA Tuition

For the 2026–2027 academic year, Syracuse Whitman lists tuition for its online master's programs at $2,086 per credit. The current Online MBA requires 45 credits, resulting in an estimated total tuition of $93,870 before applicable financial aid or other adjustments.

The university charges online master's students on a per-credit basis. Whitman also notes that online students are not charged for certain services typically associated with on-campus programs, such as campus health fees.

Because tuition rates can change between academic years, prospective students should confirm the current rate with Syracuse before enrolling.

Prior Learning and Transfer Options

The 2026 redesign introduced additional flexibility for students with substantial professional experience.

Eligible students can receive up to six credits for prior learning, with qualifying experience potentially including advanced professional responsibilities, military or veteran leadership experience, and other relevant career or life experience. Credit eligibility is evaluated individually after enrollment.

If all six credits are approved, the program could potentially be completed in as few as 39 credits rather than the standard 45.

This option is separate from traditional transfer credit and is intended to recognize relevant prior learning demonstrated through professional experience.

Online MBA Admissions

Syracuse uses a holistic admissions process for the Online MBA. The university considers academic preparation, professional experience, leadership background, and readiness for graduate study.

Applicants must hold a bachelor's degree or recognized equivalent from an accredited institution. Syracuse generally recommends a minimum undergraduate GPA of 3.0, although applicants below that level may still be considered based on factors such as graduate coursework, professional experience, leadership, quantitative preparation, and other evidence of achievement.

MBA applicants are generally expected to have at least 18 months of full-time professional experience by the time they begin the program.

Application Requirements

The current application includes several components:

  • Online application
  • Bachelor's degree or recognized equivalent
  • Academic transcripts
  • Resume or CV
  • One required essay
  • Two letters of recommendation
  • $75 application fee
  • English-language documentation when applicable for international applicants

Applicants can also submit an optional additional essay if they want to provide context that is not covered elsewhere in the application.

GMAT and GRE Requirements

The GMAT and GRE are optional for Syracuse's Online MBA. Applicants who do not submit test scores receive full consideration for admission. Candidates may submit scores if they believe the results provide additional evidence of their academic preparation.

This gives applicants flexibility in deciding whether standardized-test results strengthen their application.

International Applicants

International students can apply to Syracuse's online graduate programs. Depending on their educational background, international applicants may need to provide documentation establishing the equivalency of their academic credentials and demonstrate English-language proficiency.

Because the Online MBA can be completed entirely online, students should distinguish between online enrollment requirements and requirements associated with studying physically in the United States.

Career Support

Whitman provides Online MBA students with dedicated academic and career support. Students can receive career coaching involving areas such as career guidance, resume development, and interview preparation.

The program's specialization structure also allows students to develop knowledge connected to particular business functions. Depending on the chosen specialization, graduates may pursue opportunities in analytics, finance, marketing, operations, supply chain, accounting, entrepreneurship, or general management.

An MBA does not guarantee a particular job, promotion, or salary, and career outcomes depend on professional experience, industry, location, and individual qualifications.

Final Thoughts

Syracuse University's Online MBA has undergone a significant redesign for fall 2026, moving from 54 credits to 45 credits while retaining a core business curriculum and six specialization options. The program can be completed in as little as 15 months and can now be completed entirely online.

For 2026–2027, Whitman lists online master's tuition at $2,086 per credit, putting the standard 45-credit MBA at an estimated $93,870 in tuition before applicable adjustments or financial aid. Eligible students may receive up to six credits for prior learning.

Admission generally requires a bachelor's degree, approximately 18 months of professional experience, transcripts, an essay, two recommendations, and a resume. The GMAT and GRE are optional.
