Privacy Policy

Last updated September 1, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you visit this website and complete the verification step, and how that information is used.

Information We Collect

  • Technical data your browser sends automatically, such as IP address, browser type, device type, language and the page you came from.
  • The result of the verification step (for example, that the check was completed).
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or payment details on this website.

How We Use Information

  • To show the verification page in your language and redirect you to the requested content.
  • To protect the website against automated and abusive traffic.
  • To keep the website working, secure and measurable in aggregate.

Cookies and Local Storage

The verification page does not require cookies. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs for security and reliability. You can clear or block cookies and site data at any time in your browser settings.

Browser Notifications

Browser notifications are optional. We do not request them on this page, and if you ever allow them on another website you can disable them at any time in your browser settings.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Technical data may be processed by service providers that host and secure the website, only as needed to provide those services, or where required by law.

Data Retention

Server logs are kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting and are then deleted or anonymized.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information about you. You can exercise these rights by contacting the operator of the website.

Children's Privacy

This website is not intended for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the date at the top of this page.
