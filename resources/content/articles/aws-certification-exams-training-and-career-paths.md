AWS Certification provides professional credentials for people who want to validate knowledge and practical skills related to Amazon Web Services. The certification program covers cloud fundamentals, architecture, development, operations, data, machine learning, security, and other specialized areas.

AWS currently organizes certifications into Business, Foundational, Associate, Professional, and Specialty levels. The portfolio has also expanded into artificial intelligence, reflecting the growing use of AI and machine learning within cloud environments.

Certification is only one part of AWS's broader learning ecosystem. AWS Training and Certification also provides digital courses, hands-on labs, classroom instruction, exam preparation, Cloud Quest, and role-based learning paths through AWS Skill Builder.

## AWS Certification Levels

## Business and Foundational Certifications

AWS's foundational certifications are designed for people building general knowledge of cloud and AI concepts. The current portfolio includes AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner and AWS Certified AI Practitioner. AWS states that "no prior experience is required" for these foundational certifications.

AWS has also introduced the AWS Certified AI Business Strategist as a beta business certification, focusing on applying AI knowledge to business outcomes.

## Associate Certifications

Associate certifications validate skills for specific technical roles:
* AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate
* AWS Certified Developer – Associate
* AWS Certified CloudOps Engineer – Associate
* AWS Certified Data Engineer – Associate
* AWS Certified Machine Learning Engineer – Associate

AWS recommends prior cloud or IT experience for associate-level exams, although specific prerequisites are not mandatory.

## Professional Certifications

Professional certifications are designed for advanced AWS expertise. "Approximately two or more years of AWS experience is expected" for professional-level candidates.

## Specialty Certifications

Specialty certifications concentrate on specific technical domains. AWS announced the "retirement of the Advanced Networking – Specialty exam on December 31, 2026."

## AWS Certification Exams

AWS Certification exams are proctored assessments designed around defined job responsibilities and technical domains. Exams are delivered globally through Pearson VUE, with candidates choosing between online proctoring and testing-center appointments.

## Exam Costs

Current standard U.S. pricing:
* Business: $100
* Foundational: $100
* Associate: $150
* Professional: $300
* Specialty: $300

"AWS Certified individuals also receive a 50% discount voucher for a future exam."

If a candidate fails an exam, "AWS requires a 14-day waiting period before another attempt."

## AWS Skill Builder Training

"AWS provides 600+ free digital courses," while paid subscriptions add additional labs, practice questions, and resources. The individual subscription costs "$29 per month."

AWS offers hands-on labs and Cloud Quest, "a game-based learning experience that lets learners practice cloud concepts through role-based scenarios."

## AWS Certification Career Paths

AWS certifications support several technology career directions:
* Cloud architecture involving infrastructure design and security
* Cloud development for building and deploying applications
* Cloud operations for deploying and maintaining workloads
* Data engineering focusing on data infrastructure
* Machine learning combining infrastructure with model development
* Cybersecurity protecting AWS workloads and data

## AWS Certification and Career Development

"AWS describes its credentials as industry-recognized validation of cloud and AI knowledge and skills." However, certification should be considered alongside practical experience.

## Maintaining AWS Certification

"AWS certifications generally remain active for three years." Recertification options vary by certification, and AWS has introduced alternative maintenance pathways through Skill Builder.

## Preparation Strategy

Structured preparation combines:
* Reviewing the official exam guide
* Using AWS training courses
* Completing hands-on labs
* Practicing with official questions
* Taking official practice exams
