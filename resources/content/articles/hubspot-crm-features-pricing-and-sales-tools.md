HubSpot CRM functions as a centralized platform for organizing customer data and sales activities. The system distinguishes between its free foundational CRM tools and the paid Sales Hub product tier.

The complimentary CRM tier supports up to two users and includes "contact management, deal pipelines, CRM imports, task and activity management, reporting dashboards, and integrations." This no-cost option requires neither payment nor credit card information.

Sales Hub adds specialized capabilities for sales teams, including advanced automation, forecasting, prospecting functionality, email sequences, and sales coaching designed for organizations with "larger or more sophisticated sales operations."

Key functionality encompasses contact organization, pipeline management, task tracking, and reporting. The platform integrates with over 2,000 business applications and incorporates AI-powered features through Breeze AI, which uses a credit-based model for certain capabilities.

## Pricing

* Free: "$0 per month for up to two users"
* Starter: "$7 per seat per month when billed annually, with monthly pricing shown at $20 per seat"
* Professional: "$90 per seat per month when billed annually, or $100 with monthly billing," plus a required $1,500 one-time onboarding fee
* Enterprise: "$150 per seat per month," plus a required $3,500 one-time onboarding fee

"Pricing and promotional offers can change, so businesses should verify the current terms before purchasing."

Organizations typically select between the free CRM for basic needs or paid Sales Hub subscriptions when requiring advanced automation, forecasting, and sales management tools.
