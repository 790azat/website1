## Core Platform Description

Google Looker is a business intelligence platform within Google Cloud that emphasizes semantic data modeling. As the article notes, "Looker places significant emphasis on the underlying data model" through its LookML modeling language, enabling organizations to define consistent business metrics across analytical workflows.

## Key Capabilities

The platform supports multiple analytical functions: semantic data modeling for centralized metric definitions, data exploration interfaces allowing users to investigate information without writing SQL, dashboard creation for consolidated performance views, embedded analytics for integration into applications, and governance controls for managing access to sensitive information.

## LookML and Data Modeling

LookML is the SQL-based language used to create semantic models in Looker. This approach allows data analysts to "describe dimensions, aggregates, calculations, and relationships within a database," enabling organizations to establish business logic centrally rather than having teams recreate definitions independently.

## AI and Conversational Features

Recent updates include Gemini-powered conversational analytics. The platform now enables "users to interact with data using natural-language questions" alongside traditional dashboard navigation, representing a shift toward more dynamic analytical experiences.

## Pricing Structure

Looker offers three editions—Standard, Enterprise, and Embed—but "Google does not publish a simple universal monthly price." Organizations must contact sales for custom quotes based on platform edition and user licensing needs.
