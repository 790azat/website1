Southern New Hampshire University (SNHU) offers a broad selection of online business programs covering business administration, accounting, finance, marketing, human resources, management, project management, entrepreneurship, and other business disciplines. The university's online business school includes bachelor's, master's, associate, and certificate options designed for students with different professional and educational goals.

SNHU's online programs use eight-week undergraduate terms and 10-week graduate terms, allowing students to complete coursework without attending scheduled campus classes. The university also emphasizes transfer-friendly policies, with up to 90 undergraduate credits accepted toward eligible bachelor's programs and up to 12 credits toward most master's degrees.

Tuition is charged by credit rather than through a flat six-month term. For the 2026–2027 academic year, SNHU lists online undergraduate tuition at $354 per credit and graduate tuition at $659 per credit.

## SNHU Online Business Bachelor's Degrees

SNHU offers multiple bachelor's-level business programs. The B.S. in Business Administration is one of its broadest options, covering management, international business, collaboration, data-driven decision-making, ethical business practices, and communication.

Students can choose the general business administration track or add a concentration. Available areas include accounting, entrepreneurship, finance, human resources, marketing, project management, and other specialized subjects.

Other undergraduate business options include:
* Accounting
* Accounting and Finance
* Finance
* Human Resource Management
* Marketing
* Management
* Project Management
* Supply Chain and Operations Management
* IT Management
* Healthcare Administration

The university's online business portfolio contains more than 100 business programs and more than 15 undergraduate and graduate concentrations associated with business administration.

## Business Administration Program

The B.S. in Business Administration requires 120 credits and currently consists of 40 courses. Undergraduate courses are delivered in eight-week terms.

The curriculum combines general education, business core courses, major requirements, and electives. Students can focus on areas such as accounting or entrepreneurship while maintaining a broad business foundation.

The program is designed to develop skills including management strategies, team collaboration, data-driven business decisions, international business practices, and communication.

Students with substantial prior college coursework can potentially reduce the number of courses they need to complete at SNHU. The university allows up to 90 transfer credits toward eligible bachelor's degrees.

## Accounting and Finance Programs

Students interested in financial careers can choose dedicated accounting and finance degrees or incorporate these subjects into a broader business administration program.

The B.S. in Accounting covers financial accounting, reporting, budgeting, analysis, and related business processes. SNHU also offers an Accounting and Finance degree for students seeking a combination of accounting and financial management knowledge.

The B.S. in Finance provides a broader focus on financial analysis and business decision-making. These programs can support career paths involving corporate finance, accounting, financial services, budgeting, and analysis.

SNHU also offers graduate-level accounting and finance options for students who want to continue their education after completing a bachelor's degree.

## Marketing, Management and Entrepreneurship

SNHU's business portfolio includes several programs focused on organizational and commercial activities.

Marketing programs address areas such as consumer behavior, communication, marketing strategy, and digital business practices. Management programs provide broader preparation in leadership, organizational decision-making, and business operations.

The Entrepreneurship concentration within the B.S. in Business Administration focuses on developing business ideas, evaluating opportunities, planning, and managing new ventures. Students can use the concentration to add an entrepreneurial perspective to a broader business degree.

These programs can be relevant to students interested in corporate roles as well as people considering small-business or entrepreneurial careers.

## Project Management and Operations

SNHU also provides programs focused on managing projects and business operations.

Project management coursework can cover planning, scheduling, risk management, communication, budgeting, and project execution. These skills can apply across technology, construction, healthcare, finance, marketing, and other industries.

Supply chain and operations programs address processes involved in producing and delivering goods and services. Coursework can involve procurement, inventory, logistics, transportation, operations planning, and process improvement.

These programs can provide a more specialized alternative to a general business administration degree.

## Online MBA Programs

SNHU's online Master of Business Administration consists of 10 courses, or 30 credits, under the standard curriculum. Graduate courses are delivered in 10-week terms.

The MBA includes seven core business courses, while the remaining three courses can be electives or form a concentration.

SNHU currently offers more than 15 MBA concentrations. Examples include:
* Accounting
* Business Analytics
* Entrepreneurship
* Finance
* Healthcare Management
* Marketing
* Operations and Supply Chain Management
* Project Management
* Public Administration
* Engineering Management
* Music Business

The Business Analytics concentration, for example, focuses on descriptive, predictive, and prescriptive analytics and includes three specialized courses within the 30-credit MBA.

## MBA Tuition

For the 2026–2027 academic year, SNHU lists online graduate tuition at $659 per credit, or $1,977 for a three-credit course. A standard 30-credit MBA therefore has listed tuition of $19,770 before financial aid and applicable additional costs.

Some master's programs require 36 or 45 credits rather than 30, so total tuition varies by program.

SNHU notes that course materials can create additional costs. Graduate students may also need foundation courses depending on their previous academic background.

## SNHU Online Undergraduate Tuition

For 2026–2027, SNHU lists online undergraduate tuition at $354 per credit. A three-credit course costs $1,062, while a standard 120-credit bachelor's degree has listed tuition of $42,480 before financial aid and other costs.

Eligible active-duty military students receive a reduced online undergraduate rate of $250 per credit. SNHU also offers financial aid, scholarships, and other options that may reduce the amount a student pays directly.

The university reviews tuition rates annually, so students should confirm the applicable rate for their enrollment term.

## Transfer Credits and Potential Savings

Transfer credit can significantly affect the total cost and completion time.

SNHU accepts up to 90 credits toward eligible bachelor's programs and up to 12 credits toward most master's programs. The actual number accepted depends on the student's previous coursework and the requirements of the selected program.

For example, if 60 credits are accepted toward a 120-credit bachelor's degree, the student would have 60 credits remaining at the current undergraduate rate. At $354 per credit, those remaining credits represent $21,240 in tuition before other costs or financial aid.

SNHU evaluates transcripts to determine which previous credits can be transferred.

## Admissions and Application Process

SNHU's online application process is designed to be straightforward. The university currently states that there is no application fee, no test-score requirement, and no college essay for its online business bachelor's application.

Applicants submit the online application and provide information about their previous education. SNHU then works with students to obtain transcripts and evaluate potential transfer credits.

Graduate admissions generally require a completed bachelor's degree or equivalent prior education, with program-specific requirements varying by degree.

Because admissions requirements can change by program, prospective students should review the requirements for the exact degree they intend to pursue.

## Online Learning and Student Support

SNHU's online business programs are structured for students who need flexibility around work and other responsibilities.

Students have access to academic advising, tutoring, the Shapiro Library, and career-related resources. The university also emphasizes support throughout enrollment rather than relying exclusively on independent study.

The eight-week undergraduate terms allow students to focus on a smaller number of courses at a time, while graduate programs use 10-week terms.

## Final Thoughts

Southern New Hampshire University's online business programs cover a wide range of undergraduate and graduate subjects, from general business administration and accounting to marketing, finance, project management, supply chain management, and entrepreneurship. Its online MBA adds more than 15 concentration options, including business analytics, finance, marketing, and operations.

For the 2026–2027 academic year, online undergraduate tuition is $354 per credit and graduate tuition is $659 per credit. A standard 120-credit bachelor's degree therefore has listed tuition of $42,480, while a 30-credit MBA has listed tuition of $19,770 before financial aid and additional costs.

Transfer credits, financial aid, scholarships, and military benefits can affect the final cost. Students should compare the exact curriculum, transfer evaluation, tuition, and career objectives before selecting a program.
