## Overview

Shopify is "a cloud-based commerce platform that allows businesses to create online stores, sell products through multiple channels" and manage operations from one centralized system. It caters to entrepreneurs through large enterprises.

## Core Features

## Storefront & Products

The platform offers customizable themes and templates for building responsive websites. Merchants can add unlimited products with descriptions, images, variants, and pricing information.

## Payments & Checkout

Shopify Checkout handles transactions, while integrated payment solutions and third-party providers support cards, digital wallets, and regional payment methods.

## Inventory & Operations

"Inventory management tools" track stock across locations and sales channels, with order processing and fulfillment capabilities available through the platform.

## Multi-Channel Selling

Businesses can operate through online stores, physical locations via Shopify POS, social platforms, and marketplaces simultaneously.

## Analytics & AI

The platform includes reporting tools and Sidekick, an AI assistant helping with store management, content creation, and business analysis.

## Pricing Structure (U.S.)

* Basic: $29-$39/month
* Grow: $79-$105/month
* Advanced: $299-$399/month
* Shopify Plus: $2,300+/month

## Key Consideration

When evaluating Shopify, assess "sales volume, required channels, inventory complexity, payment costs, integrations, international operations, and the level of customization needed."
