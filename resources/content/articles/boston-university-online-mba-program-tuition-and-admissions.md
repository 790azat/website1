Boston University's Questrom School of Business offers an Online Master of Business Administration designed for working professionals seeking a flexible graduate business education. The program is delivered entirely online and awards the same Master of Business Administration degree from Boston University Questrom School of Business. (bu.edu)

The Online MBA uses an integrated curriculum rather than separating traditional subjects such as accounting, marketing, finance, and operations into independent courses. Students study interconnected business problems through six academic modules, with each module combining multiple business disciplines. The program requires 45 graduate credits and can be completed in as little as two years. (bu.edu)

The format is designed around part-time study, allowing students to remain employed while progressing through one module at a time. Weekly live sessions, asynchronous coursework, teamwork, and applied projects are incorporated into the program.

## What Is the Boston University Online MBA?

The BU Online MBA, commonly referred to as the OMBA, is offered through Questrom School of Business. The program consists of six 7.5-credit modules, plus a noncredit orientation module known as Module 0. (bu.edu)

Rather than choosing separate core and elective courses, students move through an integrated sequence focused on major management capabilities. The curriculum addresses business value creation, data-driven performance, leadership, risk, global opportunities, and innovation.

Boston University states that the program was developed using insights from more than 3,000 international industry professionals and is intended to address business challenges in a complex global environment. (bu.edu)

## Online MBA Curriculum

The six required modules provide the complete 45-credit academic curriculum.

## Creating Value for Business and Society

Module 1 examines how organizations create and capture value. Students explore concepts including economics, information economics, digital business models, and the role of managers in creating value.

The module includes an applied capstone focused on the interaction between physical products and digital services.

## Managing Performance with Data

Module 2 focuses on using data to understand and improve organizational performance. Students develop analytical and managerial skills that can support data-informed business decisions.

The module forms part of the program's broader emphasis on analytical thinking and business performance.

## Leading with Integrity

Module 3 examines leadership, organizational behavior, and responsible management. Students consider how leaders work with teams and make decisions while addressing ethical and organizational considerations.

The module contributes to the program's stated learning outcomes around responsible business leadership and collaboration.

## Assessing and Managing Risks

Module 4 focuses on identifying, assessing, and managing business risks. Students examine risk from multiple perspectives rather than treating it as an isolated financial issue.

The integrated structure connects risk considerations with broader management decisions.

## Leveraging Global Opportunities

Module 5 addresses international business and the opportunities and challenges associated with operating across markets.

Students examine how organizations can approach global opportunities while considering different economic, competitive, and organizational conditions.

## Fostering an Innovative Mindset

Module 6 focuses on innovation, entrepreneurial thinking, and organizational change. The final module completes the integrated curriculum by connecting innovation with broader management and business strategy.

Most modules include an action-oriented capstone project that requires students to apply concepts to a business situation. (bu.edu)

## Online Learning Format

Students take one 7.5-credit module per academic term. Each module is comparable to the workload of approximately two individual courses. (bu.edu)

The program combines synchronous and asynchronous learning. Weekly live sessions can include case discussions, debates, polling, interviews, and other interactive activities, while independent coursework provides flexibility around professional schedules.

Questrom recommends that students expect to spend approximately 15–20 hours per week on program activities, depending on the module and the student's preparation. (bu.edu)

Students can normally complete the six modules in six consecutive semesters, resulting in a completion time of approximately 24 months. The university also allows students to take a leave of absence when needed and states that students can have up to six years to complete the program. (bu.edu)

## Boston University Online MBA Tuition

For students beginning in Fall 2026, Boston University lists Online MBA tuition at $4,167 per module, or $25,000 in total tuition for all six modules. Students are billed at the beginning of each module. (bu.edu)

Books and course materials are not included in the tuition price. Questrom currently recommends budgeting approximately $350–$450 per semester for books and course materials. (bu.edu)

Tuition and fees are subject to change for future academic years, so prospective students should verify the current rate before enrollment.

## Financial Aid and Payment Options

Boston University provides financial-aid information for Online MBA students. Federal unsubsidized loans are available to eligible U.S. citizens and permanent residents. (bu.edu)

Questrom states that institutional scholarships are not currently available to online students, although students can pursue scholarships from external organizations. Eligible students may also have access to payment-plan options.

International students should review the financial-aid rules carefully because federal loan eligibility is generally limited to qualifying U.S. citizens and permanent residents.

## Online MBA Admissions Requirements

Applicants to the BU Online MBA submit an online application along with supporting materials.

Current application requirements include:
* Online application
* Resume
* Written essay
* Academic transcripts
* Letter of recommendation
* Proof of English proficiency where applicable

The application also includes a Kira Talent video assessment as part of the current application process. (bu.edu)

Boston University does not require applicants to submit GMAT or GRE scores. These tests are listed as optional elements of the application. (bu.edu)

Applicants should check the current application checklist because requirements and deadlines can change between admission cycles.

## Online MBA Admissions Deadlines

For the January 2027 entry, Boston University currently lists the final application deadline as October 1, 2026. Earlier rounds had extended deadlines in August and September. (bu.edu)

For the August 2027 entry, the published deadlines are February 3, March 10, and May 5, 2027, for the three application rounds. (bu.edu)

The application fee is currently $125.

Because Questrom can review applications on a space-available basis after final deadlines, applicants should verify the current schedule before submitting an application.

## Student Profile and Work Experience

The program is designed primarily for experienced professionals rather than recent undergraduate graduates.

For the Fall 2026 entering class, Questrom reports an average age of 38, approximately 12.5 years of work experience, and an average undergraduate GPA of 3.2. The class included students from both the United States and international backgrounds. (bu.edu)

The professional diversity of the cohort supports the program's emphasis on teamwork and applying business concepts to real-world situations.

## Career Resources and Alumni Network

Online MBA students have access to Questrom and Boston University career resources, including online networking platforms, recruitment resources, and the university's Center for Career Development. (bu.edu)

Students also become part of the broader Questrom and Boston University alumni communities after completing the degree.

The program can support professionals pursuing management, strategy, analytics, operations, entrepreneurship, finance, and other business-related career directions. However, an MBA does not guarantee a specific career outcome, promotion, or salary.

## Final Thoughts

Boston University's Online MBA provides a 45-credit graduate business education through an integrated six-module curriculum. Rather than dividing the degree into conventional standalone subjects, Questrom combines business disciplines around broader themes including value creation, data, leadership, risk, global opportunities, and innovation. (bu.edu)

For students starting in Fall 2026, total tuition is listed at $25,000, billed at $4,167 per module. Additional costs can include books and course materials, for which Questrom recommends budgeting $350–$450 per semester. (bu.edu)

The program is entirely online and can be completed in approximately two years when students take modules consecutively. Admission requires an application, resume, essay, transcripts, recommendation, and applicable English-proficiency documentation, while GMAT and GRE scores are optional. (bu.edu)
