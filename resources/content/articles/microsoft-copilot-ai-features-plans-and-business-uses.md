Microsoft Copilot is a collection of generative AI tools integrated across Microsoft's productivity ecosystem. The platform enables users to "draft documents, analyze information, summarize meetings, work with email, create presentations" and more.

## Key Capabilities

The service spans multiple applications:
* Word: Document drafting, rewriting, and summarization
* Excel: Data analysis through natural-language requests
* PowerPoint: Presentation creation and content structuring
* Outlook: Email summarization and response drafting
* Teams: Meeting summaries and action-item identification

Business versions access organizational data through Work IQ technology, which provides "contextual understanding based on Microsoft 365 content, metadata, connected business data" while respecting existing permissions.

## Pricing (as of publication)

* Microsoft 365 Copilot Business: $18/user/month (promotional rate through Dec 2026)
* Business Standard with Copilot: $23.50/user/month
* Business Premium with Copilot: $32/user/month
* Enterprise Copilot: $30/user/month

## Business Applications

Organizations across sales, marketing, finance, HR, and management can leverage Copilot for workflows specific to their departments, supported by prebuilt agents and customizable options through Copilot Studio.
