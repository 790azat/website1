Western Governors University (WGU) offers online business degrees designed around flexible scheduling and competency-based education. The university's School of Business provides bachelor's and master's programs in areas including accounting, business management, finance, marketing, human resources, project management, supply chain, and business-related technology. (wgu.edu)

Rather than charging students by individual course or credit, WGU generally charges a flat tuition rate for each six-month term. Students can complete as many courses as they are able to finish during that period, which makes the total cost dependent partly on how quickly they progress through their program. (wgu.edu)

WGU's programs are entirely online and use a competency-based model. Students can progress when they demonstrate mastery of the required material instead of following a traditional semester schedule with fixed classroom meetings.

## WGU Online Business Bachelor's Degrees

WGU's bachelor's portfolio includes several business disciplines.

The B.S. in Business Management covers areas such as business communication, decision-making, product development, project management, and budgeting. WGU currently reports that 61% of graduates in the program finish within 19 months. (wgu.edu)

The B.S. in Accounting focuses on accounting principles, finance, financial statements, research, and related business skills. WGU also positions the program as preparation for pursuing CPA certification, subject to applicable jurisdictional requirements.

The B.S. in Finance covers financial analysis, finance, spreadsheets, problem solving, and business management.

Other bachelor's options include Marketing, Human Resource Management, Communications, Healthcare Administration, IT Management, Supply Chain and Operations Management, Project Management, and Information Technology. (wgu.edu)

This range allows students to select a degree based on a particular business function rather than completing a general business curriculum.

## Business Management and General Business Skills

The Business Management degree is one of WGU's broader business programs.

Its curriculum addresses organizational management and includes areas such as communication, budgeting, product development, decision-making, and project management. WGU lists potential career directions including business analyst, project manager, operations manager, sales manager, supply chain manager, and entrepreneur. (wgu.edu)

The program is also structured for working adults. Students can access coursework online without attending scheduled lectures at a specific time.

## Accounting and Finance Programs

Students interested in financial careers can choose between accounting and finance-oriented programs.

The B.S. Accounting develops knowledge of financial statements, accounting systems, finance, research, and related business processes. WGU lists 41 courses in the current program and reports that 62% of graduates finish within 24 months. (wgu.edu)

The B.S. Finance is focused more broadly on financial analysis and business finance. The program currently contains 40 courses and is designed around practical financial and analytical skills.

Graduate options are also available. WGU offers an M.S. Accounting with specializations in areas including taxation, auditing, management accounting, and financial reporting. (wgu.edu)

## Marketing and Human Resources

The B.S. Marketing focuses on marketing, sales, communication, management, project management, and product management. WGU currently lists 37 courses and reports that 60% of graduates finish within 19 months. (wgu.edu)

The B.S. Human Resource Management covers recruiting, employment and labor laws, HR technology, Agile project management, and organizational practices. WGU describes the program as SHRM-recognized.

At the graduate level, WGU offers an M.S. Marketing with specializations in digital marketing or marketing analytics. The program is designed for professionals seeking more specialized marketing knowledge. (wgu.edu)

## Project Management and Operations

WGU's B.S. Project Management focuses on project planning and scheduling, risk assessment, team coordination, procurement, change management, process improvement, documentation, reporting, and project-management software. (wgu.edu)

The curriculum can also prepare eligible graduates for the educational requirements associated with the Project Management Professional (PMP) examination, although professional certification has separate eligibility requirements.

The B.S. Supply Chain and Operations Management addresses strategic planning, operations, project management, leadership, and process-related skills. These subjects can apply to manufacturing, logistics, procurement, and broader business operations.

## WGU Business Master's Degrees

WGU's School of Business also offers graduate programs.

The Master of Business Administration (MBA) covers business management, strategy, leadership, talent management, communication, financial statements, and data interpretation. WGU currently lists 11 courses and states that graduates can finish the program in approximately 12 months. (wgu.edu)

Other business master's programs include M.S. Management and Leadership, M.S. Accounting, and M.S. Marketing. The Management and Leadership program currently contains 10 courses and can be completed within 12 months according to WGU's published information. (wgu.edu)

WGU also offers specialized MBA options, including MBA programs focused on IT management and healthcare administration. (wgu.edu)

## WGU Business Tuition

For terms beginning on or after January 1, 2026, WGU lists $3,850 in tuition plus a $200 e-books and resources fee per six-month term for undergraduate business programs. Graduate business programs are listed at $4,850 in tuition plus a $200 resources fee per six-month term. WGU notes that new rates are scheduled for terms beginning October 1, 2026, so students should check the current tuition page before enrollment. (wgu.edu)

The flat-rate structure means completing additional courses during the same six-month term does not increase tuition. Students who progress more quickly can therefore potentially reduce the number of terms required to complete a degree.

Tuition also covers e-textbooks and course materials through the WGU student portal, along with course assessments and faculty support under applicable guidelines. (wgu.edu)

## Competency-Based Online Learning

WGU's competency-based model differs from a conventional credit-hour system.

Students work through course material and complete assessments when they are prepared. Someone with existing knowledge of a subject can potentially move through that course more quickly, while a student who needs additional preparation can spend more time developing the required skills.

There are no traditional scheduled lecture times for the online business programs. This structure can be useful for students balancing employment, family responsibilities, or other commitments.

WGU also assigns students a Program Mentor who provides guidance throughout the degree. (wgu.edu)

## WGU Business Admissions

Undergraduate School of Business applicants must have a high school diploma or equivalent and demonstrate program readiness through one of several routes.

Applicants can submit transcripts showing college-level coursework with a cumulative GPA of at least 2.0, hold an associate or bachelor's degree from an accredited institution, or submit high school transcripts showing a cumulative GPA of at least 2.0. (wgu.edu)

WGU does not require the ACT or SAT for admission to its business bachelor's programs. (wgu.edu)

For MBA and other business master's programs, applicants generally need a bachelor's, master's, or doctoral degree from a recognized accredited institution. WGU states that the GRE and GMAT are not required for these programs. (wgu.edu)

## Transfer Credits and Financial Aid

Students with previous college coursework can potentially transfer eligible credits into a WGU program. WGU reports that students entering bachelor's business programs transfer an average of 34 credits, although individual transfer amounts depend on previous coursework and program requirements. (wgu.edu)

WGU also provides access to financial aid and scholarships for eligible students. Federal financial aid is subject to eligibility requirements, and veterans may have access to applicable education benefits.

## Important Admission Consideration for International Students

WGU's current School of Business admissions information states that it is accepting applications only from individuals living in the United States, with limited exceptions for U.S. military personnel and their families overseas and residents of Guam, Puerto Rico, and the U.S. Virgin Islands. (wgu.edu)

Therefore, prospective students living outside the United States should verify eligibility directly with WGU before making plans around enrollment.

## Final Thoughts

WGU's online business degrees combine competency-based education with a flat-rate tuition model and flexible online scheduling. Bachelor's programs cover accounting, finance, management, marketing, human resources, project management, supply chain, IT management, and related areas, while master's options include the MBA, management and leadership, accounting, and marketing. (wgu.edu)

For 2026 terms beginning before October, WGU lists undergraduate business tuition at $3,850 plus a $200 resources fee per six-month term and graduate business tuition at $4,850 plus the same resources fee. Rates are scheduled to change for later terms.

Admissions requirements vary by degree level, and international eligibility is an important consideration because WGU currently limits applications primarily to people living in the United States. Prospective students should review the latest program, tuition, transfer, and admissions information before applying.
