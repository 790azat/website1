Informatica is an enterprise platform addressing the challenge of managing dispersed organizational data across cloud applications, databases, warehouses, and on-premises systems. The company's Intelligent Data Management Cloud (IDMC) combines multiple capabilities—"data integration, data engineering, data quality, governance, cataloging, master data management, application integration, and data access"—within a unified cloud environment.

The platform leverages CLAIRE AI technology to streamline data management workflows. Key strengths include broad connectivity across major cloud providers and enterprise applications, enabling organizations to integrate information without replacing existing infrastructure.

## Core Capabilities

The platform supports cloud data integration through ETL/ELT pipeline development and serverless processing. Data connectivity spans major providers including AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, Oracle, and Snowflake. Informatica includes change data capture functionality for continuously updated datasets and provides "data quality and observability" features to identify accuracy issues, alongside data quality profiling and monitoring.

## Data Governance and Intelligence

IDMC offers centralized cataloging with automated lineage tracking showing data movement through systems, plus governance controls for sensitive information. Master data management capabilities help organizations maintain consistent records for customers, products, and suppliers across departments. The platform features "CLAIRE AI and metadata-driven technology for automating data management activities."

## Business Applications

IDMC serves multiple business functions. Finance teams benefit from governed reporting data, sales and marketing departments can standardize customer information, operations teams monitor integrated process data, and IT manages enterprise assets systematically.

## Pricing and Evaluation

Informatica employs a "consumption-based pricing" model using Informatica Processing Units (IPUs), allowing customers to pay based on actual service usage and processing resources.

Organizations evaluating Informatica should assess integration requirements, data quality needs, governance obligations, master data complexity, AI capabilities, deployment architecture preferences, scalability expectations, and projected consumption costs.
