## Overview

Asana is described as "a cloud-based work management and project management platform designed to help teams organize tasks, coordinate projects, track deadlines, and manage workflows."

## Core Features

The platform offers task management with assignees, due dates, and dependencies. Teams can visualize work through "list views, Kanban-style boards, calendars, timelines, and Gantt charts." Additional capabilities include custom fields, workflow automation, reporting dashboards, and portfolio management for broader organizational coordination.

## Subscription Tiers

Asana offers five plans:
* Personal: Free (up to 2 users)
* Starter: $10.99/user/month (annual billing)
* Advanced: $24.99/user/month (annual billing)
* Enterprise & Enterprise+: Custom pricing

## AI and Add-ons

The platform includes "AI-generated summaries, project status assistance, smart fields, workflow creation" across paid tiers. A Timesheets and Budgets add-on costs $5.99/user/month.

## Use Cases

Organizations deploy Asana for marketing campaigns, product development, operations management, HR processes, and executive portfolio oversight to "connect everyday activities with broader organizational goals."
