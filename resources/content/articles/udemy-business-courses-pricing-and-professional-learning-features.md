Udemy Business is an organizational learning platform offering "more than 28,000 courses" for Team Plan subscribers and "more than 30,000 courses" for Enterprise customers. The platform provides professional development across technology, business, leadership, data, cybersecurity, and AI domains.

## Key Features

The platform combines extensive course libraries with administrative capabilities including "course enrollment and engagement" tracking, user management, and "skill development" analytics. It incorporates AI-powered tools like coding exercises, role-play simulations, and personalized recommendations.

For technical professionals, Udemy Business Pro adds "labs and workspaces" enabling hands-on practice in virtual environments. The platform supports "more than 200 exams" of certification preparation through standard plans, expanding to over 300 with Enterprise offerings.

## Pricing Structure

Team Plan costs "$360 per user per year" with promotional pricing at "$252 per user per year." Enterprise Plan uses custom pricing requiring direct contact with Udemy for quotes. Administrative capabilities differ significantly between tiers, with Enterprise providing single sign-on and API integrations unavailable in Team plans.

## Organizational Applications

Companies implement Udemy Business for "employee onboarding," technical skill development, leadership training, and certification preparation. The platform supports both individual self-directed learning and coordinated organizational training initiatives, making it suitable for various workforce development strategies.
