## Core Platform Features

ThoughtSpot is a cloud analytics platform centered on search and natural-language interaction. The system enables business users to ask questions about organizational data without requiring SQL knowledge. As described in the content, "Users can type questions such as revenue by region, customer growth over time, or changes in product performance."

## Key Capabilities

Search-Driven Analytics: The platform translates natural-language requests into analytical queries, returning visual answers for further exploration.

Spotter AI Agent: This agentic analytics system allows conversational interaction with business data. Rather than direct text-to-SQL generation, "Spotter translates questions into search tokens grounded in governed business definitions."

Liveboards: Interactive visualization collections that remain connected to underlying data, enabling continuous exploration beyond static reporting.

Semantic Layer: Organizations establish consistent metric definitions and business logic before queries occur, supporting governance across self-service analytics.

## Supported Use Cases

The platform serves various functions including financial analysis, sales pipeline investigation, marketing campaign performance, operational monitoring, and executive dashboards.

## Pricing Structure

ThoughtSpot Analytics pricing begins at "$25 per user per month when billed annually" for Essentials, with Pro starting at "$50 per user per month when billed annually."

## Technical Capabilities

Advanced users access Analyst Studio for data preparation, modeling, SQL, Python, and R workflows. Organizations can also embed analytics into applications using ThoughtSpot's SDK and APIs.
