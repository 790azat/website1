Microsoft Power BI is described as "a business analytics platform designed for connecting data sources, creating interactive reports, sharing dashboards, and analyzing information."

## Key Capabilities

The platform encompasses data preparation, modeling, visualization, reporting, and collaboration. Power BI Desktop serves as the development tool, while the Power BI Service handles cloud-based publishing and sharing. The system connects to over 100 different data sources, enabling organizations to consolidate information from spreadsheets, databases, and cloud services.

## Pricing Structure

Microsoft offers multiple licensing tiers:
* Free: Individual use and report development
* Pro: $14/user/month (annual billing)
* Premium Per User: $24/user/month (annual billing)
* Capacity-based options through Microsoft Fabric

## Business Applications

Different departments can leverage Power BI for distinct purposes—finance teams for budgeting and expense tracking, sales teams for revenue monitoring, operations for supply chain analysis, and HR for workforce metrics.

## Security and Integration

The platform includes row-level security, sensitivity labels, and audit capabilities. Power BI integrates within Microsoft's broader Fabric ecosystem, connecting with Excel, Teams, and Microsoft 365 tools.

Organizations should evaluate data connectivity needs, reporting requirements, security protocols, and total licensing costs when considering adoption.
