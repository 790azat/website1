Atlassian Cloud is a collection of cloud-based applications designed to help organizations manage projects, software development, business processes, documentation, service operations, and team collaboration. Its ecosystem includes products such as Jira, Confluence, Jira Service Management, Bitbucket, and Loom, alongside AI-powered capabilities from Atlassian Rovo.

The platform connects structured work management with organizational knowledge. Jira can be used to plan and track work, while Confluence provides a central environment for documentation, project information, meeting notes, policies, and other business knowledge.

Atlassian offers Free, Standard, Premium, and Enterprise plans across major cloud products. Organizations can purchase applications individually or use collections that combine multiple Atlassian tools under one subscription.

## What Is Atlassian Cloud?

Atlassian Cloud provides software-as-a-service versions of Atlassian's collaboration and work-management applications. The cloud environment is managed by Atlassian, which handles infrastructure, updates, security features, and availability.

The platform can support teams ranging from small groups to large enterprises. Jira and Confluence Free plans support up to 10 users, while paid plans provide substantially higher user limits. Enterprise plans add centralized governance and support for organizations operating at larger scale.

Organizations can also connect multiple Atlassian applications so that project information, documentation, service requests, and other work remain connected.

## Jira for Project and Work Management

Jira is one of Atlassian's primary work-management applications. Although widely associated with software development, Jira can also be configured for business projects, operational workflows, marketing activities, product management, and other structured work.

Teams can create tasks and issues, assign owners, establish priorities, define workflows, set deadlines, and monitor progress.

Jira supports different ways of visualizing work, including boards, timelines, calendars, and other planning views. Teams can also use dependencies and cross-team planning features in higher-tier plans.

## Jira Automation

Jira includes workflow automation that can reduce repetitive administrative work.

Teams can create rules that automatically assign tasks, update fields, transition issues, send notifications, or trigger other actions when predefined conditions occur.

Current Jira allowances vary by plan. Standard includes 400 automation steps per user per month, Premium provides 750, and Enterprise provides 1,000.

## Confluence for Business Knowledge

Confluence is Atlassian's collaborative documentation and knowledge-management platform.

Teams can create pages and organize them into spaces covering subjects such as:
* Project documentation
* Meeting notes
* Company policies
* Product requirements
* Training materials
* Marketing plans
* Technical documentation
* Internal knowledge bases

Confluence provides page versioning, templates, comments, permissions, databases, and collaborative whiteboards.

This makes Confluence useful for maintaining information that needs to be updated over time rather than stored as isolated documents.

## Confluence Plans

Confluence has Free, Standard, Premium, and Enterprise editions.

The Free plan supports up to 10 users and includes 2 GB of storage. Confluence Standard currently starts at $5.42 per user per month and provides 250 GB of storage, advanced permissions, audit logs, and additional collaboration features.

Confluence Premium currently starts at $10.44 per user per month and adds unlimited storage, unlimited whiteboards, additional administration and insights, Premium Support, and a financially backed 99.9% uptime SLA. Enterprise pricing is customized.

## Jira Service Management

Jira Service Management extends the Atlassian platform into IT service management and business service workflows.

Organizations can use it for service requests, incident management, change management, problem management, and internal support. Employees can submit requests through service portals while service teams manage those requests through structured workflows.

The product can also connect service operations with Jira development projects and Confluence documentation, allowing teams to relate incidents, technical work, and organizational knowledge.

## Atlassian Rovo and AI

Atlassian has integrated AI capabilities through Rovo, which includes search, chat, and AI agents.

Rovo is designed to work across organizational information and connect people, work, and knowledge. Atlassian's Teamwork Graph acts as a data intelligence layer connecting information across products such as Jira, Confluence, Bitbucket, and connected third-party tools.

On eligible plans, Rovo can help users search organizational knowledge, summarize information, and interact with specialized AI agents.

Enterprise plans provide higher Rovo usage quotas and additional governance capabilities.

## Collaboration and Knowledge Sharing

Atlassian Cloud supports collaboration through comments, mentions, notifications, shared workspaces, documentation, and integrated workflows.

A product team, for example, might use Jira to track development tasks while maintaining product requirements and decisions in Confluence. A service team could manage support requests in Jira Service Management while linking customers or employees to relevant Confluence documentation.

This connection between work items and knowledge can reduce the need to maintain separate systems for project tracking and documentation.

## Security and Administration

Atlassian provides centralized administrative controls across its cloud environment. Depending on the product and subscription, organizations can manage permissions, audit logs, data residency, user access, and other security settings.

Atlassian Guard Standard can add enterprise identity and access-management capabilities such as SSO and SCIM to eligible Standard and Premium subscriptions. Atlassian Cloud Enterprise includes Atlassian Guard Standard as part of the Enterprise offering.

Enterprise plans also provide advanced administrative controls, multiple identity providers, centralized governance, and additional security capabilities.

## Jira Cloud Pricing

Current Jira Cloud pricing provides several reference points:
* Free: $0 for up to 10 users
* Standard: $7.91/user/month
* Premium: $14.54/user/month
* Enterprise: Contact Atlassian

Jira Standard currently includes 250 GB of storage, while Premium provides unlimited storage, 24/7 critical support, and a financially backed 99.9% uptime SLA. Enterprise adds centralized governance, advanced analytics, security controls, multiple sites, and a 99.95% uptime SLA.

Atlassian's cloud pricing can vary according to product, user count, billing cycle, and usage. Monthly subscriptions can also use maximum-quantity billing for certain products.

## Atlassian Cloud Collections

Businesses that need multiple Atlassian applications can also consider collections.

The Teamwork Collection combines Jira, Confluence, Loom, and Rovo, bringing together project management, organizational knowledge, video communication, and AI-powered capabilities.

Collections can simplify purchasing when teams need several applications instead of licensing each product independently.

## How Businesses Evaluate Atlassian Cloud

Organizations generally consider:
* Project and workflow requirements
* Number of users
* Software-development needs
* Documentation and knowledge-management requirements
* IT service management
* Automation requirements
* AI usage
* Security and identity controls
* Data residency
* Storage requirements
* Integration with other business systems
* Cloud versus self-managed infrastructure

The appropriate combination depends on whether an organization primarily needs project management, knowledge sharing, service management, software development, or a combination of these workflows.

## Final Thoughts

Atlassian Cloud brings project management, documentation, service operations, collaboration, automation, and AI into a connected software ecosystem. Jira provides structured work and project management, while Confluence serves as a collaborative knowledge platform. Jira Service Management extends the ecosystem into internal and external service workflows, while Rovo adds AI-powered search, chat, and agents.

Organizations can start with individual applications and expand as their requirements grow. Standard, Premium, and Enterprise plans add progressively more storage, automation, support, security, governance, and administrative capabilities.
