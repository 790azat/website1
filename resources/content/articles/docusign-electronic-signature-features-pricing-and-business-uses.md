DocuSign is a cloud-based platform enabling organizations to prepare, send, sign, and manage documents digitally across multiple departments and business functions.

## Core Capabilities

The platform's fundamental offering centers on electronic signatures, allowing users to "upload documents, identify required signers, place signature and other fields, and send" documents for completion. Additional functionality includes reusable templates, automated audit trails documenting all agreement activity, and workflow automation that routes documents through approval chains.

For organizations with substantial document volumes, DocuSign offers bulk sending and PowerForms for self-service signing experiences. Security features encompass encryption, digital sealing, identity verification, and enterprise authentication options.

## Pricing Structure

DocuSign offers tiered pricing with annual billing options:
* Personal: $120 annually (5 envelopes/month)
* Standard: $300 annually per user
* Business Pro: $480 annually per user
* Enhanced: Custom pricing available

## Key Use Cases

The platform serves "sales, human resources, legal, finance, procurement, real estate, healthcare, and other business functions." Sales teams use it for proposals and contracts, HR departments manage onboarding materials, and finance teams handle approvals and invoices.

Organizations evaluating DocuSign should assess user count, signing volume, integration requirements, workflow complexity, and whether they need basic eSignature functionality or comprehensive agreement management capabilities.
