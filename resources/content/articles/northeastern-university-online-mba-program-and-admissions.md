Northeastern University's D'Amore-McKim School of Business offers an Online Master of Business Administration designed for working professionals who want to develop advanced business skills without leaving their careers. The program is fully online, part-time, and can be completed in as little as 18 months, while students can also choose a slower pace. (northeastern.edu)

The current Online MBA requires 50 credit hours, combining a 21-credit core curriculum with 29 credits of electives. More than half of the program can therefore be customized around a student's professional interests, with opportunities to develop expertise in areas such as artificial intelligence, business analytics, finance, marketing, and supply chain management. (northeastern.edu)

Northeastern also emphasizes experiential learning. Students complete courses based on real business challenges, including an experiential decision-making course and a societal challenge experience involving industry-oriented projects.

## What Is the Northeastern Online MBA?

The Northeastern Online MBA is a part-time graduate business program delivered entirely online through the D'Amore-McKim School of Business. The school is accredited by the Association to Advance Collegiate Schools of Business (AACSB). (northeastern.edu)

The program is structured for professionals who want to study while continuing to work. Students can complete the degree in approximately 18 months on an accelerated schedule, while Northeastern states that many students take about 2.5 years and that the program can be extended for greater flexibility. (northeastern.edu)

The curriculum combines on-demand learning with optional live faculty sessions and office hours, allowing students to interact with instructors and classmates while retaining flexibility for independent study.

## Online MBA Curriculum

The 50-credit curriculum is divided into 21 core credits and 29 elective credits. This gives students substantial flexibility in selecting courses that correspond with their professional interests. (northeastern.edu)

## Core Business Courses

The core curriculum establishes knowledge across major business functions.

Students study areas including:
* Accounting
* Financial management
* Marketing
* Operations
* Organizational management
* Data analysis
* Strategy
* Entrepreneurship

The curriculum is designed to connect these disciplines to broader business decisions rather than treating each function independently.

For example, financial information can be considered alongside strategic decisions, while operations, customer relationships, innovation, and organizational dynamics are examined as interconnected elements of business performance. (northeastern.edu)

## Experiential Learning

Experience-powered learning is a major component of Northeastern's Online MBA.

One required course, Experiential Business Decision Making, places students in a simulated executive environment where they work as part of a team to make strategic decisions under changing business conditions. Students collaborate with industry leaders and receive feedback on their recommendations. (northeastern.edu)

Students also complete an EXPO Societal Challenge experience. Available challenges can involve subjects such as sustainability, international strategy, ESG investing, and diversity, equity, and inclusion.

These experiences are designed to connect academic concepts with situations that resemble problems faced by organizations.

## Electives and Areas of Specialization

Because 29 of the 50 credits are electives, students have considerable control over the direction of their degree.

Northeastern allows students to choose a concentration or build expertise through elective courses within an area of interest. The university currently highlights options including AI Applications for Business, business analytics, finance, marketing, and supply chain management.

## Artificial Intelligence

The AI-focused curriculum addresses the application of artificial intelligence in business rather than concentrating exclusively on technical model development.

Students can examine how organizations use AI for decision-making, innovation, operations, and business transformation.

## Business Analytics

Analytics coursework focuses on using data to understand business performance and support decisions. This can be relevant to professionals working with reporting, forecasting, business intelligence, and data-driven strategy.

## Finance and Marketing

Finance electives can provide additional knowledge in financial decision-making and corporate finance, while marketing coursework can support professionals working in customer strategy, branding, digital marketing, and commercial management.

## Supply Chain Management

Supply chain coursework addresses operations, sourcing, logistics, and the management of complex business processes. It can be relevant to professionals working in procurement, operations, manufacturing, and distribution.

## Online Learning Format

The Online MBA combines asynchronous course materials with opportunities for live interaction.

Each course provides optional weekly live faculty sessions, as well as faculty office hours. Students can therefore participate in real-time discussions while completing the majority of their coursework according to their own schedules. (northeastern.edu)

Northeastern also offers optional in-person experiences, including campus events and certain travel-based electives. These are not required for students completing the degree entirely online.

The program can be completed in as little as 18 months, while students who prefer a lighter course load can extend their studies. Northeastern currently states that students can take up to seven years to complete the program. (northeastern.edu)

## Northeastern Online MBA Tuition

For students entering during the 2026–2027 academic year, Northeastern lists the Online MBA tuition rate at $923 per credit. With the standard 50-credit curriculum, this produces an estimated tuition cost of approximately $46,150 before additional expenses or applicable financial assistance. (northeastern.edu)

Northeastern's Online MBA page currently displays an estimated total tuition of approximately $46,150. Tuition rates can be revised by the university, so prospective students should confirm the applicable rate for their intended start term. (northeastern.edu)

For Fall 2026, Northeastern also lists a 14% Online MBA scholarship for eligible students who enroll through the Standard Application. The university states that this scholarship is automatically applied and does not require a separate application. (northeastern.edu)

## Online MBA Admissions

Northeastern offers two primary admission pathways for the Online MBA: the Fast App Performance-Based Admissions pathway and the Standard Application. (northeastern.edu)

The Fast App is designed to let prospective students demonstrate readiness through actual graduate-level coursework rather than completing a traditional application process.

Applicants begin with a simplified application and enroll in two designated pathway courses. They must earn a B or better in both courses and provide proof of a completed bachelor's degree before formally matriculating into the MBA program. (northeastern.edu)

## Standard Application Requirements

Applicants who prefer traditional admissions can use Northeastern's Standard Application.

The current requirements include:
* Application form
* Personal statement of up to 500 words
* Resume
* Two letters of recommendation
* Academic transcripts
* Completed bachelor's degree
* Minimum 3.0 GPA from an accredited institution
* Optional GMAT or GRE scores
* English-language testing when applicable

Northeastern states that "GMAT and GRE scores are optional for the Online MBA." (northeastern.edu)

Applicants using the Standard Application are fully admitted before beginning the program, unlike Fast App students who must first complete the pathway courses.

## International Applicants

International applicants can apply to the Online MBA, but Northeastern recommends that applicants whose bachelor's degree was earned outside the United States use the Standard Application so their academic credentials can be reviewed before enrollment. (northeastern.edu)

The Online MBA is not F-1 visa eligible because it is an online program. International applicants should therefore review Northeastern's requirements for international transcripts and English-language proficiency before applying.

## Career Development

Northeastern's Online MBA is designed to develop skills applicable to a range of management and business careers.

The flexible elective structure allows students to build knowledge around their existing profession or a potential career transition. Areas such as analytics, finance, marketing, AI, operations, and supply chain can provide additional specialization within the broader MBA.

The experiential courses also provide opportunities to practice decision-making, teamwork, communication, and problem-solving using business scenarios.

An MBA does not guarantee a specific job, promotion, or salary. Career outcomes depend on professional experience, industry, location, individual qualifications, and employer requirements.

## Final Thoughts

Northeastern University's Online MBA provides a flexible, fully online graduate business program through the D'Amore-McKim School of Business. The current curriculum requires 50 credits, with 21 credits of core coursework and 29 credits of electives, allowing students to customize a substantial portion of the degree. (northeastern.edu)

For 2026–2027, tuition is listed at $923 per credit, resulting in an estimated $46,150 for the standard 50-credit program before additional costs or financial assistance. Eligible students entering through the Standard Application can receive Northeastern's listed 14% Online MBA scholarship. (northeastern.edu)

The program offers two admissions routes, including a performance-based pathway that allows students to demonstrate readiness through two graduate courses. The Standard Application requires a bachelor's degree, 3.0 GPA, personal statement, resume, recommendations, and transcripts, while GMAT and GRE scores are optional. (northeastern.edu)
