## September 24, 2026 • Daniel Brooks

ADP payroll services provide businesses with cloud-based tools for processing employee pay, calculating and filing payroll taxes, managing workforce information, and supporting human resources activities. ADP serves businesses ranging from very small companies to organizations with thousands of employees, with different products designed around workforce size and operational requirements.

For small businesses, RUN Powered by ADP provides payroll, tax filing, employee self-service, onboarding, and optional HR services. Larger organizations can use broader ADP workforce management and human capital management solutions with more extensive payroll, HR, benefits, time, and analytics capabilities.

The platform combines payroll automation with compliance support and additional workforce services, making it possible for businesses to manage payroll alongside related employee processes.

## What Are ADP Payroll Services?

ADP provides payroll software and services for businesses of different sizes. Its small-business offering, RUN Powered by ADP, is designed for organizations with approximately 1–49 employees, while ADP also provides payroll solutions for businesses with 50 or more employees.

The payroll system can calculate employee wages and deductions, handle applicable payroll taxes, generate tax forms, and support different payment methods.

Businesses can also connect payroll with accounting, time tracking, HR, benefits, retirement, and other workforce applications.

## Key ADP Payroll Features

## Payroll Processing

ADP allows businesses to process payroll online and supports recurring payroll automation. RUN & DONE can automate recurring payroll for eligible businesses, while administrators can also make additional payroll runs when necessary.

The platform supports different employee payment options, including direct deposit, ADPCheck, and Wisely Direct for eligible users.

Businesses can manage payroll information through an online dashboard that displays upcoming payroll dates, alerts, and tasks.

## Payroll Tax Filing

Payroll tax administration is a major component of ADP's services. ADP calculates, files, deposits, and reconciles applicable federal, state, and local payroll taxes for supported payroll services. The company also states that it responds to inquiries from tax agencies concerning filings and deposits it handles.

This can reduce the administrative burden associated with payroll compliance, particularly for businesses operating across multiple jurisdictions.

## W-2 and 1099 Forms

ADP can create and deliver W-2 and 1099 forms and electronically file applicable forms with the IRS. The current RUN comparison indicates that year-end forms are included as a payroll feature, although additional fees can apply.

This allows businesses to manage employee and contractor reporting through the same payroll system.

## Employee Self-Service

ADP provides employee self-service tools through ADP Employee Access. Employees can view pay history, access tax documents, and manage certain payroll-related information online.

Self-service can reduce routine questions for payroll administrators because employees can access commonly requested information without requiring manual assistance.

Employees can also complete certain onboarding tasks electronically, including entering personal information, providing direct-deposit details, and completing Form W-4.

## Time and Attendance

ADP can connect payroll with time and attendance systems. Businesses can use these tools to manage employee schedules, hours worked, and time off before transferring relevant information into payroll.

For hourly organizations, this integration can reduce manual calculations and provide a more consistent process between recorded hours and payroll.

Time and attendance is available as an add-on for the small-business RUN packages rather than being included in every base package.

## HR and Employee Management

ADP extends beyond payroll into broader HR management. Depending on the package, businesses can access onboarding, HR tracking, background checks, recruiting tools, employee documents, HR support, and compliance resources.

Complete Payroll & HR Plus includes features such as HR HelpDesk, an employee handbook wizard, HR tracking, forms and documents, and HR training resources. The HR Pro Payroll & HR package adds enhanced HR support, applicant tracking, learning management, and additional employee services.

This structure allows companies to add HR capabilities without necessarily moving to a completely separate platform.

## Benefits and Retirement Services

ADP also provides access to benefits and retirement services that can be connected with payroll.

Available options can include health insurance, workers' compensation, retirement plans, and other employee benefits. These services may be offered as add-ons depending on the payroll package.

Integrating benefits and payroll can simplify deductions and provide businesses with a more centralized approach to workforce administration.

## Multi-State Payroll and Compliance

Businesses with employees working in different states or local jurisdictions can use ADP's multi-jurisdiction payroll capabilities.

RUN's current package comparison states that businesses can pay employees working across multiple states or local jurisdictions within the same pay period while ADP handles applicable tax filing.

This can become increasingly important as companies hire remote employees or expand into additional states.

## ADP Payroll and AI

ADP has incorporated AI into its payroll tools through ADP Assist. The current RUN platform includes "an AI-powered virtual assistant that can answer payroll and HR questions and a payroll inspector that flags potential errors before payroll is finalized."

ADP also provides AI-assisted smart timesheet import functionality, allowing businesses to import employee hours from existing timesheet files.

These features are intended to reduce repetitive administrative work and identify potential payroll problems before they affect employees or tax filings.

## ADP Payroll Pricing

ADP does not publish a universal flat monthly price for its payroll services. Instead, the company directs businesses to request pricing based on their workforce, selected package, and required services.

For small businesses, RUN Powered by ADP currently offers four primary packages:
* Essential Payroll: Core payroll, tax filing, compliance support, and employee access.
* Enhanced Payroll: Adds services such as State Unemployment Insurance management, garnishment payment services, and recruiting functionality.
* Complete Payroll & HR Plus: Adds basic HR support, HR tracking, documents, and training resources.
* HR Pro Payroll & HR: Adds enhanced HR support, applicant tracking, learning management, and additional HR services.

Additional services such as time and attendance, retirement, workers' compensation, and health insurance can be added depending on business requirements.

Because ADP uses customized pricing, businesses should consider the complete package, employee count, additional services, and payroll frequency when calculating total costs.

## ADP for Growing Businesses

ADP's product structure allows businesses to start with smaller payroll solutions and move toward broader workforce management platforms as they grow.

A company with only a few employees may primarily need payroll and tax filing. A growing organization may eventually require recruiting, benefits administration, time tracking, HR support, workforce analytics, and multi-state payroll.

ADP's broader business payroll services are designed for organizations with 50 or more employees and can provide more configurable payroll and HR functionality.

## How Businesses Evaluate ADP Payroll

Businesses evaluating ADP should consider employee count, operating states, payroll frequency, hourly versus salaried workers, contractor payments, benefits, time tracking, HR requirements, and integration needs.

Pricing should be assessed together with optional services because the base payroll package may not include every capability a company requires.

Businesses should also evaluate implementation, employee self-service, customer support, tax compliance processes, and how easily the payroll system can integrate with accounting and other workforce applications.

## Final Thoughts

ADP payroll services combine payroll processing, tax filing, employee payments, reporting, and workforce administration in a cloud-based environment. RUN Powered by ADP provides a small-business-focused option, while ADP also offers broader payroll and HR solutions for larger organizations.

The platform's capabilities extend beyond basic payroll through employee self-service, time tracking, benefits, HR services, multi-state payroll, and AI-assisted administration.

For businesses evaluating ADP, the most important considerations are workforce size, required payroll and HR features, operating jurisdictions, additional services, integrations, and the customized cost of the selected solution.
