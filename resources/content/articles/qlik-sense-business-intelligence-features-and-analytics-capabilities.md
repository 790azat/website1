Qlik Sense is a business intelligence platform centered on associative analytics that enables organizations to explore data relationships beyond predefined reporting paths. The platform serves both technical users building data models and business users exploring information through dashboards.

## Core Capabilities

The platform's defining feature is its associative engine. As the content explains, "when users make selections in an application, the platform recalculates visualizations and shows relationships between selected, associated, and unrelated information."

Key features include:
* Data connectivity across files, databases, cloud services, and business applications
* Interactive visualizations allowing direct selection within charts and tables
* Data preparation and transformation capabilities
* AI-assisted analytics through Insight Advisor, enabling natural-language searches and automated insight generation

## Deployment Options

Qlik offers two main deployment models:
* Qlik Cloud Analytics: SaaS-based environment supporting collaboration and governed access
* Qlik Sense Enterprise: Client-managed solution for enterprise-scale requirements

The platform also supports embedded analytics through APIs and SDKs for integration into custom applications.

## Evaluation Factors

Organizations should consider data source compatibility, analytics needs, deployment preferences, governance requirements, scalability, and current pricing models when evaluating Qlik Sense for their business intelligence environment.
